Local demo setup

This page has zero JS of its own. The only voice-related bit is a single <script src="voice.js"> tag injected after you paste a JWT below. In a real CRM, your backend mints and templates the JWT before the page is rendered.

Mint a JWT against the running Tring API:

KEY='tk_test_dev_local_only_change_me_in_prod'
AGENT=$(curl -sS -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" http://localhost:8085/v1/agents | jq -r '.[0].id')
curl -sS -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" http://localhost:8085/v1/agents/$AGENT/token | jq -r .token

What's happening under the hood

  • Each Call button has data-tring-call data-to="…" — that's the whole integration on the HTML side.
  • voice.js fetches your agent's SIP credentials with the JWT, registers sip.js over WSS against FreeSWITCH on :7443, and listens for incoming INVITEs.
  • Clicking Call posts to /v1/sdk/calls. Tring tells FreeSWITCH to ring the agent's browser; on answer, the call bridges to the destination via the configured trunk (loopback in dev, Exotel/Airtel/Jio/Tata in production).
  • The floating panel in the bottom right shows ringing → connected → ended status with a single Hang up button. Pure CSS, no framework.
