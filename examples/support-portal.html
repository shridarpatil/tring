🛟 Acme Help Center

Customer-facing support page. The visitor has no account — they tap Call support and land in our IVR over the internet (WebRTC). The only voice code is one <script src="support.js"> at the bottom.

Local dev setup: grab the tenant's public widget key, then load the widget.

curl -sS -H "Authorization: Bearer $CONSOLE_JWT" http://localhost:8085/v1/guest-config | jq -r .public_key

(Make sure the tenant has a support_flow_id set AND this page's origin allowlisted — PATCH /v1/guest-config with {"support_flow_id":"<flow uuid>","allowed_origins":["http://localhost:9999"]}. Without a matching allowed_origins entry the mint returns 403 — that's the anti-key-theft guard.)
